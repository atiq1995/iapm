# Five-minute executive demonstration

0:00–0:35 — Data Refinery. “Our proposition is to convert historical engineering evidence into traceable asset and investment decisions. These records are independently generated for this private demonstration. Extraction is illustrated here; a live pipeline is a pilot integration.”

0:35–1:10 — Bad actors. Select Safa Coast and review actual business loss. Open the highest-ranked asset. “Every headline returns to contributing incident evidence. Potential exposure remains separate from actual loss.”

1:10–1:45 — Asset 360 and incident history. Show recurring failure modes and recorded maintenance. “The engineering mechanism determines what intervention deserves evaluation. Missing operating exposure is disclosed instead of producing a misleading MTBF.”

1:45–2:20 — MEDFI. Show a D-stage F2 observation and its earlier matching F1 events. “Stage and impact attribution are separate. A condition that reaches F is recorded as a linked progression, preserving its history.”

2:20–3:20 — DCBF. Select the dominant asset. Compare A1, A2, B and C, then change CAPEX or effectiveness. “All alternatives share a status-quo baseline and evaluation horizon. These are illustrative forecasts, not achieved savings.” Inspect cash-flow export and replacement timing.

3:20–4:15 — Capital portfolio. Reduce the budget. “This transparent heuristic prioritizes candidate interventions within available capital. Mandatory obligations are escalated if they exceed funding. Shared outage dependencies remain a pilot refinement.”

4:15–5:00 — Actions. Create an evidence-linked inspection or engineering review with an owner and due date. “Value realization requires an accountable action and verified completion. A controlled pilot would validate data coverage, operating exposure, model assumptions, integration and role segregation before operational use.”
